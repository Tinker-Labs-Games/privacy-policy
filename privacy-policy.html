Privacy Policy

Tinker Labs (Dror Cohen) • Last updated: December 31, 2025

Who we are. This Privacy Policy explains how Dror Cohen, doing business as Tinker Labs (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile games and related services (the “App” or “Services”). We are based in Israel.

Contact. Email: privacy@tinker-labs.com

1) Quick summary

  • We use information to operate the game, keep it secure, measure performance, and (depending on your choices/region) show and measure ads.
  • You can manage optional data processing inside the app: Settings → Ads & Privacy.
  • Some partners (like advertising providers) may process data under their own privacy policies.
  • You can request access/deletion and other rights by emailing us.

2) What we collect

A. Information you provide

  • Support and feedback. If you contact us or send in-game feedback, we receive what you submit (for example: message text, and optionally an email address if you choose to include one). Anything you type may contain personal data if you choose to include it.

B. Information collected automatically (device/app)

  • Identifiers. Device and app identifiers (for example: IP address; app instance identifiers; device model; OS version; language; time zone; approximate region). On iOS, use of the advertising identifier (IDFA) depends on your App Tracking Transparency choice.
  • Gameplay and usage. Events about how the App is used (for example: screens viewed, actions taken, session length, and feature usage).
  • Crash and diagnostics. Crash reports, performance metrics, and diagnostic logs.
  • Approximate location. We may infer an approximate location (for example, city/region) from IP address or signals used by ad/analytics providers. We do not ask you to provide precise GPS coordinates for gameplay.
  • Purchases. If you buy something, we receive confirmation/receipt data from the app store platform to validate the purchase and deliver items. Payment details are handled by the app store (for example, Apple or Google).

C. Account and login

  • We support sign-in via platform services (for example, Google Play Games or Game Center) and may also use an anonymous account identifier tied to your device for gameplay continuity.

D. Session replay (only if enabled by your choices)

  • If you allow it in Settings → Ads & Privacy, we may collect interaction data to understand usability issues (for example: taps, navigation timing, and screen flow). This may include replay-style data of your session. Please avoid entering sensitive information into free-text fields.

3) How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App. Save progress, enable account features, deliver purchases, and provide requested support.
  • Security and fraud prevention. Detect abuse, prevent invalid traffic, and protect our Services.
  • Analytics and improvement. Understand performance, fix bugs, and improve gameplay and UX.
  • Advertising and marketing measurement. Show ads, measure campaign effectiveness, and attribute installs/purchases. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

4) Our legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA/UK, we rely on different legal bases depending on the purpose:

  • Contract (to provide the App and requested features).
  • Consent (for optional processing where required, such as certain analytics, session replay, and personalized advertising).
  • Legitimate interests (for example, basic service operations, security, fraud prevention, and measuring performance, where permitted).
  • Legal obligations (for example, compliance and recordkeeping where required).

5) When we share information

We share information with service providers and partners to run the App. Examples include:

  • Advertising: Google AdMob, Meta (Facebook) and related partners.
  • Analytics and infrastructure: Google Analytics 4 / Firebase.
  • Game backend: Microsoft PlayFab.
  • Session replay: Smartlook (only if enabled by your choices).
  • Crash reporting: Unity crash reporting tools.
  • Consent management UI: the in-app consent dialog shown via Settings → Ads & Privacy.

Important: Some partners (especially advertising providers) may process certain data as independent controllers for their own purposes described in their privacy policies, such as delivering and measuring ads and preventing fraud. Your choices in Settings → Ads & Privacy affect what is enabled in the App.

6) Your choices: Settings → Ads & Privacy

  • You can open Settings → Ads & Privacy at any time to accept or reject optional categories and adjust your choices.
  • On iOS, you can also control tracking permission via the system prompt and device settings (App Tracking Transparency).
  • You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings (availability depends on OS).

7) California notice (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have rights to know/access, delete, correct, and opt out of certain disclosures of personal information.

  • Do Not Sell or Share: if we “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, you can opt out by using Settings → Ads & Privacy (rejecting personalized advertising/marketing-related categories where available).
  • How to submit requests: email privacy@tinker-labs.com. We operate exclusively online, so we provide email as a request method.

8) Retention

  • Account and gameplay data: kept while you use the App, plus a reasonable period for backup, fraud prevention, and support.
  • Crash/diagnostics and analytics: typically retained up to 24 months (or less if configured), then deleted or aggregated/anonymized where possible.
  • Support/feedback: typically retained up to 24 months after last contact, unless we need longer to resolve a dispute or comply with law.

9) International transfers

We are based in Israel and may use service providers that process data in other countries (including the United States and EU). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers (for example, standard contractual clauses used by providers).

10) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

11) Your rights

  • You may have rights to access, delete, correct, object, restrict processing, and data portability, depending on where you live.
  • If processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time via Settings → Ads & Privacy.
  • To exercise rights, contact privacy@tinker-labs.com.

12) Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above and, if changes are material, we may provide additional notice in the App or on our website.
